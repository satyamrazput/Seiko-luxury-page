Shinshu, Japan

Where the water
never stops moving.

In the heart of a primeval forest in the Shinshu region of central Japan flows the Tateshina Waterfall. In this same region, the elite craftspeople of the Micro Artist Studio create the Spring Drive watches of the Grand Seiko Masterpiece Collection.

This manual-winding wristwatch, crafted by these very artisans, conveys time's eternal flow in nature as it unfolds through this mystic waterfall — captured not in a photograph, but in hand-cut steel.

The Mechanical Truth

Caliber 9R02, without embellishment.

No complications borrowed for effect. Every figure below is a specification of the movement inside this case — built, regulated, and finished by hand at the Micro Artist Studio.

01

Manual-Winding Spring Drive

Caliber 9R02, hand-assembled and adjusted at the Micro Artist Studio, Shiojiri.

02

Dual-Spring Barrel

Two mainsprings housed within a single barrel for sustained, even torque delivery.

03

Torque Return System

Recaptures spring energy that would otherwise be lost, extending the power reserve.

04

84-Hour Power Reserve

Power reserve indicator moved to the case back to preserve the dial's uninterrupted beauty.

Micro Artist Studio

Thirty-nine jewels. No shortcuts.

This manual winding Spring Drive movement is produced entirely within the Micro Artist Studio. The Dual-Spring Barrel and Torque Return System work together to achieve a power reserve of approximately 84 hours — enough to remove the watch on Friday evening and find it still running come Monday.

Craftsmanship

Three surfaces. One idea of water.

The dial, the case, and the strap were each engraved and cut to express the same motion — water finding its way through stone.

The Dial
A dial expressing gushing spring water

Engraved by hand, line by intersecting line.

The hand-engraved dial comes to life through craftsmanship of the highest order. Its engraved lines run in multiple directions, intersecting in a seamless flow to capture the essence of spring water emerging from the earth.

The hour and minute hands, and each hand-applied, diamond-cut marker, are made of 14k white gold — sharp, brilliant, and legible even as the tempered gray Spring Drive seconds hand glides silently across the textured surface.

The Case
A 44GS case engraved with fluid lines

Countless lines, cut to catch every angle of light.

Echoing the flow of water depicted on the dial, countless hand-engraved lines inspired by the mystic waterfall adorn the 44GS case. Radiating patterns flow seamlessly across the sharp ridgelines that define the 44GS design.

Zaratsu polishing and brushed finishing on the Platinum 950 case, combined with its delicately engraved surfaces, further enhance the shifting beauty of light and shadow.

The Straps
Two straps, two expressions of water

Crocodile leather, or the shimmer of splashing water.

The watch is offered with two straps. A classically styled black strap, made of crocodile leather both outer and lining, finished with glazing on the outer leather.

  • Alternate strapLeather with metallic accents by KYOTO Leather, inspired by splashing water
  • ClaspThree-fold clasp with push-button release, Platinum 950 (partly 18k white gold)
  • IncludedAn extra KYOTO Leather strap in the box
